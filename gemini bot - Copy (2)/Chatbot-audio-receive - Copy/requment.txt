Project Flow With File Names


whatsapp_mes_rece.py
Receives WhatsApp messages through the Flask webhook.

Audio_repy_analy.py
Downloads the audio file and saves it in the audio_file folder.

ai.py
Sends the audio to Gemini and extracts ticket details.

Template_send.py
Sends the ticket preview to WhatsApp for confirmation.

ai.py
Temporarily stores the ticket details in pending_tickets.

whatsapp_mes_rece.py
Receives the user’s yes or no reply.

ai.py
Creates or cancels the ticket according to the user’s reply.

insert.py
Creates the Issue in ERPNext and uploads the audio file.

whatsapp_send_text.py
Sends the final success or error message to the user.

security.py
Reads API keys and URLs from the .env file.

number.json
Stores registered WhatsApp numbers and names.

web.py
Provides the Streamlit admin panel to add, search, and delete numbers.

main.py
Fetches customer information from ERPNext.

log.py
Saves message and reply logs.
